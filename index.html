About

I believe the best solutions happen when creativity, technology, and empathy come together to solve real problems.

I am a multidisciplinary designer interested in how ideas move from possibility to something people can actually use. I approach each project with curiosity, asking not only how something should work, but why it should exist and how it should feel to interact with.

My work moves between digital and physical experiences, allowing me to think beyond a single medium or discipline. I enjoy exploring a problem from different angles, experimenting with possibilities, and shaping those discoveries into clear, intentional solutions.

I am especially drawn to the space where design and emerging technology meet, and to creating work that feels thoughtful, useful, and human.
